PodRelay for Windows

PodRelay on iPhone needs a companion app on your Windows PC. You tap on your iPhone, and the companion app connects or disconnects your headphones on the PC over Bluetooth.

How it works

  1. 1 · PodRelay on iPhoneTap a button in the app, a Lock Screen widget or a control.
  2. 2 · Your local networkThe request goes straight to your own PC on the same network, encrypted with a key created when you pair. No cloud service.
  3. 3 · PodRelay for Windows on the PCConnects or disconnects the headphones that are already paired with the PC, and reports the result.

PodRelay only changes the PC's connection. It can't make your iPhone release or take over the headphones. If they are in use by another device, iOS and the headphones decide what happens.

System requirements

PCWindows 10 version 2004 (build 19041) or later, or Windows 11. 64-bit (x64). Bluetooth with Windows' built-in Bluetooth audio support. No .NET installation needed.
iPhonePodRelay 1.0.0 for iOS 18 or later.
NetworkiPhone and PC on the same local network. The network must be set to Private in Windows.
HeadphonesBluetooth headphones that are already paired with the PC. AirPods Pro and AirPods Max are recognized automatically.

Tested devices

Pair with your iPhone

  1. In the Windows app's settings, next to Remote control from iPhone, click Open….
  2. Turn on Allow remote control on the local network.
  3. Click Allow in firewall… and approve once. The rule only allows this app, TCP port 47833, private networks and your local subnet.
  4. Click Pair iPhone…. A QR code appears. It is valid for 2 minutes and can be used once.
  5. Open PodRelay on your iPhone, tap Scan QR Code and allow local network access when iOS asks.

Already have the Windows app? Open PodRelay and go straight to pairing.

Troubleshooting

PodRelay says it can't reach the PC

Check that the PC is awake, that PodRelay for Windows is running (icon next to the clock), that remote control is on, and that the iPhone is on the same network. In Windows, the network must be Private. Guest networks often block devices from reaching each other.

The headphones don't connect

Open the case or take the headphones out, and keep them near the PC. If they are playing audio from another device, they may not switch. Choose them in iPhone's Control Center if you want them back on iPhone.
