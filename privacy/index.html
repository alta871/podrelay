Privacy Policy

PodRelay sends no data to us or to third parties. The iPhone app only talks to your own PC on your local network, and everything it needs is stored on your own devices.

Effective 31 October 2026 · Applies to PodRelay for iPhone 1.0.0, PodRelay for Windows 1.1.0 and this website.

Who is responsible

Astrit Morina, Göteborg, Sweden. Email: info@astrit.se.

Summary

  • No account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting services in the apps.
  • The apps don't send anything to us or to any server on the internet.
  • The iPhone app and the Windows app only communicate with each other, on your local network, after you pair them.
  • You can delete everything: “Forget PC” and deleting the app on iPhone, and uninstalling on Windows.

Sent to your PC

When you pair: an ID for the iPhone and the device name iOS gives apps (normally just “iPhone”). For each request: the command (for example “connect” or “disconnect”), where it came from (app, widget, control or shortcut) and security data such as random numbers and a message counter. Requests are encrypted with the key created when you paired. Nothing leaves your local network.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan the QR code shown by the Windows app when pairing. Images are processed on the iPhone and never stored or sent.
  • Local network: to reach your PC.

The Windows app doesn't contact the internet and has no automatic updates. With remote control turned on, it only accepts connections from your local network. To delete the data, uninstall the app and choose to delete settings, or remove a single iPhone in the app's remote control window.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party resources. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which, like any web host, processes your IP address and browser information to deliver the pages and protect the service. See GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.)'s privacy statement.

App Store and Apple

Apple handles your App Store download and purchase under Apple's privacy policy. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous usage and crash statistics. We receive nothing that identifies you.

Email to support

If you email us, we use your email address and what you write only to answer you. We keep the conversation as long as needed for that, and no longer than 12 months. We don't share it with anyone.

Your rights

We don't hold personal data about you, other than any email you send us. You can ask us what we have, have it corrected or deleted, and object to our handling, by emailing info@astrit.se. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Sweden that is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

Children

PodRelay collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the apps start handling data differently, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
